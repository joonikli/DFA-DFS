# Working Style

Prioritize accuracy and faithful execution over speed.

Figma file to reference: https://www.figma.com/design/jIXKjfx96sNdtcKZgMBeI6/Dress-for-Success-Redesign?node-id=781-12667


Before changing anything:
1. Inspect the relevant existing files, screens, components, and prior work.
2. Explain your understanding of the intended result in 2 to 4 concise bullets.
3. Identify assumptions, missing information, or conflicting patterns.
4. If an unclear decision would materially affect the result, ask before implementing it.

When working:
- Treat existing work as the source of truth.
- Continue established visual, interaction, naming, and organizational patterns.
- Do not redesign, simplify, or alter unrelated areas unless requested.
- Make the smallest complete set of changes needed.
- Do not invent requirements or pretend something works when it has not been verified.
- Check neighboring screens, states, and flows before deciding how something should behave.
- Preserve work completed by other team members.
- Work through one clearly defined section at a time.

After working:
- Review the result against the original request.
- Test every interaction or state you changed.
- Clearly state what was completed, what was assumed, and anything that still needs review.

Communication:
- Be thoughtful, direct, and concise.
- Explain reasoning when multiple solutions are possible.
- If my proposed approach has a meaningful usability or technical problem, point it out instead of agreeing automatically.
