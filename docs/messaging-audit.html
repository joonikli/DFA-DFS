Messaging flow audit

Dress for Success volunteer manager prototype · Nov 1, 2026 · Covers direct messages, compose, email, announcements, threads and unread state

Messaging handles the happy path, but I reproduced 13 bugs in the prototype and found 39 edge cases with no designed state. Three should be fixed before anything else. Group emails put every volunteer's address in To:. A failed send has no state at all. The volunteer profile panel disappears on any screen 1440px wide or narrower, which includes most 13–14" laptops.

Update, prototype v2: all 13 bugs are fixed. Also shipped from the plan: the failed-send state with Retry and "Update email", Delivered and Seen receipts, private group email, per-conversation drafts with Discard, the channel picker moved into the composer with a channel filter in the list, a "New message" pill, toasts for replies that arrive on other pages, "Seen by N of M" with a Nudge button on announcements, email announcements, roster cards and the profile drawer. Still open: scheduling and reminders, templates, SMS, editing announcements, and group management.

Message lifecycle

The prototype models three states: Draft, Sending and Sent. A real build needs the dashed states. The failure branches (red) matter most, because volunteers act on shift logistics.

In prototypeProposedFailure state, proposed

Each state needs a visible treatment in the bubble's footer. For example: a spinner with "Sending…", a red "Not sent · Retry" link, and a "Seen by 18 of 23" line on announcements.

Open decisions

These are product calls the design has to make before the edge cases above can be specced. Each has my recommendation.

  1. Is "In-app / Email" a filter or the send channel?It sits in the filter row but only changes the composer. Recommendation: move the channel picker into the composer, and turn the filter-row control into "All channels / In-app / Email".
  2. One thread per person, or one per channel?Recommendation: one thread per person, with a channel icon on each message. Bridget thinks in people, not channels.
  3. Can announcements go to individuals?Recommendation: no. Individual recipients become a group message, which keeps announcements tied to a group that has a member list.
  4. Do we add texting (SMS)?The draft copy "Text me if you need anymore help" implies it, and volunteers check texts before they check email. Recommendation: yes, as a third channel with opt-in consent. Plan it for P2.
  5. Group email: separate copies or BCC?Recommendation: separate copies, because they personalize better and replies go back to one thread.
  6. Do volunteers see read receipts?Recommendation: managers see "Seen by" on announcements, and volunteers never see receipts on direct messages.
  7. Edit, delete and retention rules, including volunteers under 18.Recommendation: a 15-minute edit window for messages, editable announcements with an "Edited" label, a 30-day soft delete, and staff copied on any thread with a volunteer under 18.
